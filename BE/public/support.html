DayFlow — Support

Questions or problems? Email support@dayflow.app — we reply within 2 business days.

My alarm did not ring

Allow notifications for DayFlow (Settings → Notifications). On iOS, keep the ringer volume up and avoid force-quitting the app; when DayFlow is in the background the alarm is delivered as a notification — tap it to open the alarm and complete your mission.

How do wake-up missions work?

When an alarm with a mission rings you must solve math problems, repeat a memory pattern, type a phrase, shake the phone, walk, solve a puzzle or scan a registered QR code / barcode before it turns off. If the QR code cannot be scanned you can switch to a hard math mission after 45 seconds.

Google Calendar

Connect or disconnect in Settings → Google Calendar. DayFlow only reads your events.

Delete my account

Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all data.
