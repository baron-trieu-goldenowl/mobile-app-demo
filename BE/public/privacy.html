Camera images (QR mission) and motion-sensor readings (shake / walk missions) are processed on the device and never stored or uploaded. We do not collect location, contacts or advertising identifiers, do not track you, do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and do not sell or share your data.

2. Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar we request the read-only scope calendar.readonly, used only to display your events inside DayFlow. DayFlow's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Disconnect any time in Settings → Google Calendar; imported events are deleted and the token is revoked.

3. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account exists. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all data immediately and revokes Google / Apple tokens.

4. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest.

5. Children

DayFlow is not directed to children under 13.
